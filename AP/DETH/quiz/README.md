# Practice quiz

[practice-quiz.pdf](practice-quiz.pdf): 45 minutes, 5 questions. One small MDP (a coffee cart) is used in all
questions: the model and the Markov property, the return, Bellman equations for a fixed policy, one step of policy
iteration, and two sweeps of value iteration. Allowed: a simple calculator. Marks are given for reasoning, not for the
final number alone.

[practice-quiz-answers.pdf](practice-quiz-answers.pdf): the final answers, to check your own work. Try the quiz first.
