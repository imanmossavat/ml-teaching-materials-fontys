# Orientation – Inspiration Session

This directory contains the foundational materials for the first session of the semester. The goal of this session is to provide a broad overview of Artificial Intelligence and encourage collaborative brainstorming regarding its real-world impact.

## Contents

* **Introduction to Artificial Intelligence_distributed.pptx**: The introductory lecture presentation mapping out the landscape of AI, its history, and potential.
* **Make_a_poster_session_1.docx**: The assignment brief for your first group project. In this session, you will work in teams to design a poster exploring an AI-related topic, application, or ethical challenge.

## Learning Objectives

* Gain a high-level overview of AI and its current capabilities.
* Collaborate with peers to critically analyze and present an AI application or concept via a poster session.
